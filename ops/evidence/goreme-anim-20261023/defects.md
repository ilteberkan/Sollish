# Göreme animation audit (23 Oct 2026)

Method: local `next dev` (:3000). Rounds were forced through the dev preview hook `__goremePreview.play(seed, mode)` (frames) and through stubbed entropy plus a real Spin click (fps, browser spec). Engine results were not changed. CDP screencast frames were sorted by frame timestamp (CDP delivers them out of order) and aligned to timeline beats by wall clock. Scenarios: plain spin, ways win, a 4-lift chain with a fly-away, a merge, a 2-sun tease, a 3-sun trigger with feature intro, free spins with moorings, sky ticks and callouts, the total screen, Büyük, Mega, Süper, Efsane and Azami, turbo, quick, reduced motion, at 1440×900 and 390×844. Seeds are in `web.json`.
